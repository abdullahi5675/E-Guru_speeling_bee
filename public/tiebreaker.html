Tiebreaker Cockpit on Standby

This arena remains locked during normal stage rounds. It is only opened when the Judges complete an attempt round and launch a tiebreaker session from End Stage & Review.

📋 How the Tiebreaker Activates:
  1. Judges conduct the stage round in the Judges Cockpit.
  2. When all spellers have spelled and the round is finished, click 🏁 End Stage & Review (in the top header or below the arena).
  3. If multiple contestants have the same score at the advancement cutoff, the system detects the tie.
  4. Click ⚡ Send Tied Students to Tiebreaker Cockpit to initialize this arena.
